Field manual

What you actually need to know.

01

Toll-free the whole way

  • The pass: Col du Petit-Saint-Bernard (2,188 m) is toll-free and open in summer (reopened 22 May 2026; usually closes ~early Nov). Single-lane alternating sections — pad time.
  • Avoid the tunnels: Fréjus and Mont Blanc tunnels are ~€56 each way. The A43/A32 autoroute–autostrade are tolled. Stay on D / SS / SP roads.
  • Italy: only "A"-numbered autostrade (A4, A22…) are tolled. SS11 and the lakeside SS roads are free — slower, but scenic.
  • No Switzerland: with Lugano dropped, the route stays in France & Italy — so there's no Swiss motorway vignette to worry about at all.
02

EV range & battery plan

  • Car: Model Y 2022, 75 kWh · ~280 km full-charge on non-motorway roads at ≤90 km/h.
  • Rule: never below 20%, charge to 80% → ~168 km usable per cycle. Plan keeps the driving minimum ~24%.
  • Sleeping in the car drains ~15–20% overnight (Camp-mode AC + Sentry in July heat). So the plan tops up near each overnight to sleep at ~75% and wake at ~55% — well above the 40% go-to-bed buffer, and never near the floor by morning. The Venice hotel night differs: the car sits ~2 days at your Mira spot while you bus in, and Sentry mode (the security cameras) still draws ~7–14% per 24 h whenever the car is left unattended — so it drops ~15% (~56% → ~41%); you top up on the Day-6 drive out (Padova → Garda). Turn Sentry off in a safe spot to keep more. (Sentry also nibbles a few % during long daytime stops — absorbed by the per-day buffer.)
  • No overnight charging is assumed (Park4Night spots rarely have a plug) — the evening fast-charge near each overnight is what carries you through the ~20% drain.
  • Mountain climbs burn ~1.5×; the long descents regen a big chunk back. Charge to 80% before each pass (Aosta side / Albertville side).
  • Verify the day-of status of the Aosta, Cavaglia and Albertville chargers (single points of failure on the two mountain days).
03

Free swims, no tourist traps

  • Maggiore: the south shore around Angera / Ranco (by the Busto Arsizio Supercharger), not Stresa / Isola Bella.
  • Como (two days): swim quiet at Lezzeno & the Cernobbio lido; villas & views at Villa del Balbianello and Bellagio's Punta Spartivento — go early, before the Bellagio / Varenna crush.
  • Garda: Torri / Brenzone & the north end, not Sirmione / Limone.
  • Most beaches are pebble — bring water shoes. Arrive before ~10:00 (free-beach parking fills on hot days).
04

Venice with a car (+ access fee)

  • No car enters Venice. From your Mira spot, leave the car parked (free) and take the ACTV bus 53E straight to Venezia Piazzale Roma (~45 min, ~€3.50–4.50 each way; daily, with a late night service), then a 5-min walk to the Hotel Carlton by the station. Far cheaper than the island garages (Tronchetto / Piazzale Roma, ~€25–35/day) and no Mestre parking needed.
  • In the city, a Venezia Unica pass covers the vaporetti (incl. Burano / Murano); otherwise pay singles (vaporetto ~€9.50 a ride). The 53E bus to/from Mira is a separate ACTV ticket (~€3.50–4.50 each way).
  • Access Fee: in 2026 it applies on dated peak days (Apr 3 – Jul 26, 08:30–16:00, €5–10) — your dates are inside it.
  • You're exempt as overnight hotel guests, but it isn't automatic: register the exemption and carry the QR voucher from Venezia Unica for your stay dates.
  • Booked: Hotel Carlton On The Grand Canal, Santa Croce 578 — on the Grand Canal right by Santa Lucia station / Piazzale Roma (5-min walk from the 53E bus, vaporetto stop outside); ~10 min to the Frari, ~12 min to Rialto. Venice's magic is at dawn and after the day-trippers leave.
