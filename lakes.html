Field manual

What you actually need to know.

01

D-road navigation

  • In Tesla nav, set Avoid Highways in route preferences (or use ABRP with the same setting).
  • Country D-roads average 60–70 km/h with stops · expect ~1.5× the time vs autoroute for the same distance.
  • Watch for tractors, slow trucks and village 30 km/h zones · accept it, that's the trip.
02

Lake practicalities

  • Lac Pavin: swimming allowed but no facilities · 92 m deep · cold even in August (~18°C max).
  • Lac du Salagou: motor boats banned · sailing & swimming free · the warmest of the three.
  • Lac de Sainte-Croix: Galetas beach is the kayak rental hub · launch at 9–10 am to beat afternoon wind.
03

Charging strategy

  • Tesla SC network covers all transit days even on D-roads (most SCs are in town centers off the autoroute).
  • Inside the volcano area: Issoire SC is closest to Pavin (40 km).
  • Mountain D-roads = +20% range buffer.
  • End days at ≥40% if using Camp Mode.
04

Budget anchors (per person)

  • Charging: ~€170. 2,300 km ÷ 225 km × €16 ≈ €164
  • Tolls: €0 (no autoroute = no péage)
  • Verdon kayak rental: €20
  • Food (cook + occasional bouchon/bistro): €150–200
  • Total ~€350–400 per person, 10 days · the cheapest of the three trips

Three lakes, three days off, no autoroute, no rush.

The Auvergne is volcanic, the Salagou is Martian, the Verdon is Caribbean. The trip is built so each gets a full rest day of swimming and walking — the journey between them is on the slow roads through medieval villages, vineyard country, and lavender fields.
