Field manual

What you actually need to know.

01

Pass openings

  • Col de l'Iseran: open early-July to late-September only · check inforoute73.fr before D4
  • Cormet de Roselend: open mid-May to October
  • Lac du Mont-Cenis: open year-round but pass road only May–November
  • If Iseran is closed: alternative is Col du Mont-Cenis (2,083 m, lower but also dramatic)
02

EV range & battery plan

  • Start state: leave Chambéry at 80%
  • Overnight loss: ~20% from Camp Mode AC. Each SLEEP step shows arrival % and wake %.
  • Hard floor: 15%. Trip is planned so the minimum reachable state is always ≥30% for buffer.
  • Mountain driving with elevation gain consumes ~25% more · regen recovers ~30% on descent
  • Charge to 90% before the Iseran pass · pre-condition battery on cold mornings above 2,000 m
03

Sleeping legality (France)

  • Sleeping inside a parked car is legal almost everywhere · camping (chairs out) is what's banned
  • Most alpine D-roads have signed lay-bys with no overnight ban
  • Avoid signposted "interdit aux camping-cars" zones near busy resorts (Val d'Isère in season)
  • Park4Night has dense coverage in this region
04

Budget anchors

  • Charging: ~€64. 142 kWh delivered · 75 kWh battery × ~€0.45/kWh Supercharger
  • Per-stop: D1 Annecy €7.8 · D2 Albertville €10.8 · D3 Bourg-SM €13.8 · D4 Bourg-SM €19.6 · D5 St-Jean €12.2
  • Tolls: €0 · all D-roads + free A43 sections
  • Food: ~€80 (cook + 2 mountain meals)
  • Vanoise NP entrance: free
  • Total ~€145 · 5 days
