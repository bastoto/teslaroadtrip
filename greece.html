Wild Europe · Tesla Model Y · Sleeping in the car

Down the Adriatic, into Greece, and back.

A 30-day overland loop from Poissy through the Dolomites, Slovenia, Croatia, Montenegro and Albania, into Greece — then back up through Bosnia and the Slovenian Alps. No ferries. Every night sleeps in the car for free; every leg ends near a fast charger.

Field manual

What you actually need to know.

01

Border & paperwork

  • Schengen: France → Italy → Slovenia → Croatia. Passport checks rare.
  • Non-Schengen: Montenegro, Albania, Bosnia. Passport at every border.
  • Green Card insurance: request it free from your French insurer for Montenegro, Albania, Bosnia.
  • Vignettes: Slovenia (digital, ~€16/week), Croatia (toll motorways).
02

Sleeping legality

  • Italy / Montenegro / Albania / Bosnia: very tolerant. Discretion = no issues.
  • Slovenia / Croatia: officially restricted to campsites, but tolerated outside tourist zones if discreet.
  • Greece: wild camping is technically illegal but tolerated outside NPs and signposted beaches.
03

Apps to install

  • Park4Night (paid tier) — main app
  • iOverlander — secondary, especially in the Balkans
  • ABRP — better than Tesla nav for cross-network charging
  • PlugShare — essential CCS app for Bosnia & Albania
  • maps.me — offline maps for patchy data zones
04

Charging strategy

  • End every day with ≥40% if you use Camp Mode (5–10% per night)
  • +20% range buffer in Dolomites, Albanian Alps, Lovćen
  • Albania: only 1 Tesla SC (Tirana) + CCS in Shkodër & Saranda
  • Bosnia: zero Tesla SC. CCS in Mostar & Sarajevo. Always cross at 90%+.
05

Sleeping setup

  • Fold Y rear seats flat — bed area ~1.95 m × 1.10 m
  • 5 cm foam topper over the seat-back gap
  • Full window covers including glass roof (Tesmanian/Basenor)
  • Magnetic bug nets, two windows cracked 1 cm
  • Sentry mode off at night, "Dashcam only" on
06

Budget anchors (per person)

  • Charging: ~€600. 8,400 km ÷ 225 km per 20–80% × €16 ≈ €608
  • Tolls (Italian autostrade): €180
  • Tunnels (Mont Blanc both ways): €100
  • Vignettes (Slovenia ~€16/week): €30
  • Food (cook + occasional taverna): €400–500
