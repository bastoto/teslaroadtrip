From the Atlantic to the Mediterranean, all coast.

A 30-day coastal loop from Poissy down France's Atlantic edge, around the green cliffs of Spanish Cantabria, through Galicia and the Portuguese cliffs to Cabo de São Vicente, then back along the Spanish Mediterranean. Free overnight spots, fast charging, no hotels.

Field manual

What you actually need to know.

01

Borders & paperwork

  • All-Schengen route: France → Spain → Portugal → Spain → France. No stops at any border.
  • Just carry passport + Carte Grise + insurance card.
  • No vignettes anywhere on this trip. Spanish & Portuguese motorways are tolled (electronic transponder optional).
02

Sleeping legality

  • France: sleeping inside a parked car is legal almost everywhere. Camping (chairs out) is what's banned.
  • Spain: "pernocta" (sleeping in vehicle) is legal but "acampar" (camping outside vehicle) is not. Be discreet — windows up, no chairs.
  • Portugal: banned in protected areas since 2021. Use Park4Night for verified-safe spots; avoid signposted "no overnight" zones especially in the Algarve.
03

Apps to install

  • Park4Night (paid tier) — main app, very dense in Iberia
  • iOverlander — secondary
  • ABRP — better than Tesla nav for end-to-end charging
  • Tide tables (e.g. Tides Near Me) — essential for As Catedrais beach
  • Via Verde Portugal — pre-pay tolls via app
04

Charging strategy

  • Tesla Supercharger network is excellent in all 3 countries · no CCS needed
  • Coastal road sections (Asturias, Galicia, Algarve cliffs) often have no SC for 100+ km — top up before going onto small coastal roads
  • End every day with ≥40% if you use Camp Mode (5–10% per night)
  • Iberia summer heat is real — Camp Mode A/C burns more than in Greece
05

Sleeping setup

  • Fold rear seats flat — bed area ~1.95 m × 1.10 m
  • 5 cm foam topper over the seat-back gap
  • Full window covers including glass roof — Iberian sun is brutal
  • Magnetic bug nets, two windows cracked 1 cm
  • Sentry mode off at night, "Dashcam only" on
06

Budget anchors (per person)

  • Charging: ~€500. 6,800 km ÷ 225 km × €16 ≈ €484. Spain is the cheapest country in EU for SC.
  • Tolls (Spain + Portugal motorways): €120
  • French autoroute tolls: €80
  • Île de Ré bridge: €8
  • Food + tapas/pintxos + occasional taverna: €450
  • Total ~€1,150–1,300 per person, 30 days

Two oceans, four months of summer in 30 days.

The Iberian Peninsula has more coastline than any other in Europe. This trip touches almost all of it — Atlantic on the way down, Mediterranean on the way back, with the western edge of the continent in between.
